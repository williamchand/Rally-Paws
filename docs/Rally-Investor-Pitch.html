RALLY · PRODUCT PITCH01
The opportunity

Our active lives do not fit in one tracker.

01

Too many silos

Sport, workouts, health data, meals, venues and friends can live in separate places.

02

One-size metrics

Time and calories miss the measures players care about: pace, score, skill and personal bests.

03

Hard to return

Logging can feel like admin. People need useful recommendations and rewards for ordinary days.

These are product hypotheses to validate, not established market claims.
